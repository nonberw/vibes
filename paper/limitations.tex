\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

The pre-registered out-of-range estimator could not decide H2, H4 or the locus rule: free-flight
stretches are short at high gravity, and at low gravity the curvature fit is biased by bounces the
model predicts and the reference never makes. This is the main failure of the study design. The
law fraction, the WIDE ceiling and the reading of a split failure were introduced after the first
evaluations were seen; they are untested
on fresh data and uncorrected for multiplicity. Three seeds (one for WIDE, LRU and size) estimate
seed variance poorly. The world is a 2D toy with small two-stage models; we make no claim about
large video generators. The high-side claim for STATE rests on one validated linear write that
inversion does not corroborate. Appendix~\ref{app:limits} expands each point.
