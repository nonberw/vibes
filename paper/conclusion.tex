\section{Conclusion}

Under squared loss, clamping an inferred parameter to the training range is the optimal response to
noisy evidence. Our models did not clamp: they went about half-way to the law above the range and
stayed near the clamp below it, and their beliefs under-read out-of-range gravity similarly. We
found no single locus. Inference and execution both fail, and our pre-registered instruments were
too noisy to separate them. Next: more seeds and an estimator fixed in advance.
